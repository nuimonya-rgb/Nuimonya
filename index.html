REPAIR SERVICE

Made to Be Loved for Longer

At Nuimonya, we want you to enjoy your handmade pieces for many years to come. That's why we offer free repairs for items purchased from our shop.

  • Contact us firstIf your bag or accessory needs repairing, please contact us first. We'll assess its condition and let you know whether the repair is possible.
  • Free of chargeRepair costs are free of charge.
  • By postIf you are unable to bring your item to us in person, you can send it to us by post. Customers are responsible for the shipping costs both ways: sending the item to us and returning it after repair.
  • A little giftAs a small thank-you for continuing to cherish and use your Nuimonya piece, we'll also include a little gift with your repaired item.

We hope you will continue to enjoy your Nuimonya creations for many years to come.

Please note that some repairs may not be possible, depending on the condition of the item and the type of repair required.
